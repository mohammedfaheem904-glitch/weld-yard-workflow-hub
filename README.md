# Weld Yard Workflow Hub

Build a new corporate website for **Weld Yard — Integrated Welding Solutions**.

### 1. Business Positioning

Weld Yard is an integrated welding solutions company providing:

- Welding Products
- Welding Engineering
- QA/QC & Inspection
- Digital Welding Management

Main tagline:

**Weld Yard — Integrated Welding Solutions**

Supporting message:

**Products. Engineering. Inspection. Digital Welding Management.**

### 2. External Store — Important

The online store is a separate, existing WordPress + WooCommerce website.

Store URL: **https://store.weldyard.com**

Do NOT build an ecommerce store inside this Lovable project.

Do NOT create product databases, shopping carts, checkout pages, payment processing, or order management.

Instead, create a professional Products section on the corporate website that introduces our product categories and directs visitors to the external WooCommerce store.

All Shop, Browse Products, and View All Products buttons must link to:

https://store.weldyard.com

Where appropriate, product category links may point to existing store category URLs once verified.

Open the external store in the same browser tab unless there is a clear usability reason to do otherwise.

### 3. Separate DWM Application

Weld Yard DWM is also a separate application.

URL: **https://dwm.weldyard.com**

Do NOT rebuild the DWM application or its backend inside this project.

Create a dedicated DWM landing section explaining its value and features, with clear CTAs linking to the DWM website.

Use the DWM URL only for relevant application and demo links.

### 4. Website Pages

Create:

- Home
- Solutions
- Welding Products
- Welding Engineering
- QA/QC & Inspection
- Weld Yard DWM
- Industries
- About Weld Yard
- Contact
- Request a Quote
- Request a DWM Demo

### 5. Homepage

Hero headline:

**Integrated Welding Solutions**

Supporting text:

From welding products and engineering support to quality inspection and digital welding management, Weld Yard connects the essential stages of your welding operations.

Primary CTA: Explore Our Solutions

Secondary CTA: Contact Our Team

Include four main business areas:

1. Welding Products
2. Welding Engineering
3. QA/QC & Inspection
4. Digital Welding Management

Add a visual welding lifecycle:

Material → Engineering → Welder Qualification → Welding → Inspection → Quality Documentation → Handover

Include dedicated sections for industries served, company capabilities, DWM benefits, and contact conversion.

### 6. Navigation

Use this navigation:

Home | Solutions | Products | DWM | Industries | About | Contact

Add a prominent **Visit Store** button linking to https://store.weldyard.com.

Add a separate **Request DWM Demo** CTA linking to the appropriate DWM demo or contact flow.

### 7. Brand & Design

Use the Weld Yard brand identity with #FF914D as the primary accent color.

Create a modern, premium industrial B2B experience with strong typography, relevant industrial imagery, clear content hierarchy, responsive layouts, and accessible navigation.

Avoid making the corporate website look like a generic ecommerce storefront.

### 8. Technical Requirements

- Responsive and mobile-first
- SEO-friendly pages and metadata
- Semantic HTML and accessible components
- Fast loading and optimized images
- Reusable components and clean architecture
- Functional navigation and contact forms
- Prepare for English and Arabic localization
- Do not invent company certifications, client logos, project references, or performance statistics

### 9. Deployment Safety

Build this as an independent new project.

Do not modify the existing WordPress store, the DWM application, their databases, DNS settings, or the current production website.

The corporate website will eventually be deployed to https://www.weldyard.com after testing and approval.

Focus on the corporate website, service positioning, content architecture, and UI/UX. Do not implement the external store or DWM application inside this project.
